# AI Use Cases for Smart Zoo Platform

## Overview

The Smart Zoo platform leverages Artificial Intelligence to enhance visitor experience, improve operational efficiency, support conservation efforts, and ensure animal welfare. The following use cases have been prioritized based on business value and feasibility.

---

## 1. Visitor Analytics

### Objective
Understand visitor behavior, preferences, and movement patterns throughout the zoo.

### AI Capabilities
- Analyze visitor traffic using camera feeds and mobile app data.
- Generate heatmaps for visitor movement.
- Identify popular attractions and underutilized areas.
- Measure exhibit dwell times.

### Inputs
- CCTV/Smart cameras
- Mobile application location data
- Ticketing systems

### Outputs
- Visitor journey insights
- Peak-hour analytics
- Attraction popularity reports

### Business Benefits
- Improved visitor experience
- Better attraction planning
- Optimized staffing and facility management

---

## 2. Crowd Prediction & Crowd Management

### Objective
Predict crowd density and proactively manage congestion.

### AI Capabilities
- Real-time crowd detection.
- Forecast crowd formation using historical patterns.
- Alert staff when crowd thresholds are exceeded.
- Recommend alternate routes for visitors.

### Inputs
- Video feeds
- Event schedules
- Weather data
- Historical attendance trends

### Outputs
- Crowd density maps
- Risk alerts
- Predictive traffic forecasts

### Business Benefits
- Improved safety
- Reduced waiting times
- Better visitor satisfaction

---

## 3. Animal Health Monitoring

### Objective
Detect early signs of illness or abnormal behavior.

### AI Capabilities
- Behavior analysis using computer vision.
- Activity tracking and anomaly detection.
- Pattern recognition for health assessment.
- Automated veterinary alerts.

### Inputs
- Camera feeds
- Wearable sensors
- Veterinary records

### Outputs
- Health risk scores
- Behavioral anomaly alerts
- Monitoring dashboards

### Business Benefits
- Improved animal welfare
- Early disease detection
- Reduced veterinary intervention costs

---

## 4. Feeding Monitoring & Compliance

### Objective
Ensure feeding schedules are followed and nutritional requirements are met.

### AI Capabilities
- Detect feeding activities.
- Validate feeding schedules.
- Measure feeding frequency and duration.
- Track food consumption trends.

### Inputs
- Camera feeds
- Feeding schedules
- Keeper records

### Outputs
- Compliance reports
- Missed feeding alerts
- Consumption analytics

### Business Benefits
- Improved animal nutrition
- Better operational compliance
- Reduced manual tracking

---

## 5. Population Monitoring & Conservation

### Objective
Maintain accurate animal population records.

### AI Capabilities
- Species recognition.
- Animal identification.
- Automated counting and census generation.
- Breeding and migration pattern analysis.

### Inputs
- Camera feeds
- RFID/Sensor data
- Conservation records

### Outputs
- Population reports
- Growth trend dashboards
- Conservation insights

### Business Benefits
- Improved reporting accuracy
- Better conservation planning
- Simplified regulatory compliance

---

## 6. Personalized Visitor Recommendations

### Objective
Provide tailored experiences to zoo visitors.

### AI Capabilities
- Recommend attractions based on interests.
- Create optimized visit routes.
- Suggest events and activities.
- Recommend food courts and amenities.

### Inputs
- Visitor profile
- Previous interactions
- Real-time location
- Crowd information

### Outputs
- Personalized itinerary
- Event recommendations
- Navigation assistance

### Business Benefits
- Increased visitor engagement
- Improved customer satisfaction
- Higher revenue opportunities

---

## 7. AI-Powered Zoo Assistant

### Objective
Provide intelligent assistance to visitors and staff.

### AI Capabilities
- Natural language interaction.
- Knowledge retrieval through RAG.
- Multilingual support.
- Context-aware assistance.

### Visitor Scenarios
- Animal information
- Directions and navigation
- Event schedules
- Ticket information

### Staff Scenarios
- Operating procedures
- Maintenance information
- Incident management support

### Business Benefits
- 24x7 assistance
- Reduced support workload
- Faster information access
